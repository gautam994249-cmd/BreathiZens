Ginger Root Extract:

Ginger, or Zingiber officinale, is a widely used plant valued for its distinctive flavor and naturally occurring compounds, including gingerols. It appears in foods, beverages, and some herbal supplements. In BreathiZen, ginger root extract forms part of the botanical blend intended for general wellness. Research into ginger's compounds has examined several biological effects, but findings from laboratory research or studies using different preparations do not establish the benefits of this particular spray. Consumers should review the label and seek professional advice when they have concerns about suitability.
3

Bromelain Powder:

Bromelain is an enzyme complex derived mostly from pineapples, usually their stems. This enzyme is used in certain dietary supplements and can have different biological activities. According to BreathiZen, one of the five components of this dietary supplement is bromelain powder. Presence of this enzyme is not enough to say that this product can clear mucus and lungs congestion or can treat certain disease of respiratory system. People with pineapple allergy must be more cautious about using such a product and consult their doctors in advance. Additionally, bromelain can interact with other medications, such as blood-thinning drugs.

Organic Lemon Extract:

Lemon, known botanically as Citrus limon, is widely used in food and wellness products because of its refreshing flavor and naturally occurring plant compounds. Organic lemon extract is included in the marketed BreathiZen formula. The word “organic” describes how an ingredient is produced under applicable standards; it does not independently establish that the ingredient is more effective for respiratory health. Lemon-derived ingredients can contribute to a product's flavor and composition, but their presence does not demonstrate that the finished spray strengthens the lungs or prevents respiratory infections. Consumers with citrus sensitivities should review the full ingredient list and check with the manufacturer if they are unsure about potential allergens or other components.

Organic Cordyceps militaris:

Cordyceps militaris is a species of fungus used in some traditional wellness practices and modern dietary supplements. It has attracted research interest because it contains various naturally occurring compounds, although evidence depends on the preparation, dosage, and outcome being studied. BreathiZen lists organic cordyceps as part of its botanical blend. People who take medication or have an underlying health condition should discuss cordyceps supplements with a qualified healthcare professional before use. Consumers should also check the label for the exact extract, amount, and any additional ingredients.

Bromelain Powder:

Bromelain is an enzyme commonly obtained from pineapple and used in some dietary supplements.
